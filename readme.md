# Curse of the Sea

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/icons/app.ico" alt="Curse of the Sea" width="128" height="128">
</p>

A linear, story-driven pirate adventure built from scratch in **C++23** and **DirectX 12**.

You play a pirate father searching the cursed seas for his lost daughter: sailing through haunted waters,
forsaken ports, and whatever the tide drags up.

## Tech

- C++23
- DirectX 12
- Custom engine (rendering, platform layer, asset pipeline: all hand-rolled)
- Windows-only

## Status

Early development.
